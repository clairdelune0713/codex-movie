# Tart & Tine: A Little Braver Together

**Production screenplay — the completed movie is `exports/tart-and-tine-60s-final.mp4`.**

60 seconds • 16:9 • warm whimsical 3D inspired by Up • no dialogue

Two guide-dog puppies in training discover that friendship means lending your courage, then accepting a little help in return.

## 00–10s — Two little trainees

Meet Tart and Tine; a gentle game begins.

**00–03s:** Tart and Tine sit together in garden, ears perked. Tart glances shyly at Tine; Tine answers with a bright tail wag.
Camera: 35mm wide at puppy eye height; slow push toward both puppies. Sound: Soft breeze, distant birds; original playful piano motif begins.

**03–06s:** Tine lowers a forepaw onto ball and gently nudges it along the gravel path; Tart tilts the head in delight.
Camera: 50mm medium two-shot; paws and faces in one coherent composition. Sound: Soft rubber tap and gravel tick; pizzicato joins piano.

**06–10s:** Tart and Tine trot after ball through garden toward the near bridge ramp. Tine leads by half a body length.
Camera: 35mm lateral tracking, consistent left-to-right travel. Sound: Light paw patter; vest cloth flutter; upbeat motif.

## 10–20s — A tiny big obstacle

The ball crosses a small bridge that feels very big to Tart.

**10–13s:** ball rolls slowly over the low wooden bridge in garden and settles in grass beside the far-side daisies.
Camera: 50mm ground-level follow; show near ramp, bridge crown, far ramp and destination. Sound: Gentle wood roll, then soft grass rustle.

**13–16s:** Tine crosses the bridge confidently and stops beside ball. Tart reaches the near ramp and slows.
Camera: 35mm wide side view; both ramps visible; no axis crossing. Sound: Paw taps on wood; piano skips upward.

**16–20s:** Tart places one paw on the first bridge plank in garden, hears a tiny creak, and withdraws the paw. Ears droop; eyes look across toward Tine.
Camera: 65mm close-up from near-side three-quarter view; bridge stays low and safe. Sound: A small wood creak; a soft canine breath; score thins to two piano notes.

## 20–30s — Coming back

Tine notices the hesitation and chooses friendship over the ball.

**20–23s:** Tine looks from stationary ball to Tart across the bridge in garden. Playful expression softens into concern.
Camera: 65mm medium close-up; rack focus from ball to Tart across bridge. Sound: Bird ambience; piano holds a warm chord.

**23–26s:** Tine walks back across the bridge in garden, right to left, slowing as the puppy reaches Tart on near-side gravel.
Camera: 35mm wide profile maintains the established axis; return direction visibly motivated. Sound: Measured wooden paw taps; gentle pizzicato pulse.

**26–30s:** Tine gently touches noses with Tart and waits. Tart raises the ears, takes a steadying breath and returns the nose touch.
Camera: 85mm close two-shot at nose height; tiny slow push. Sound: Quiet sniff and soft exhale; a tender original piano phrase.

## 30–40s — One paw at a time

They cross together, at Tart’s pace.

**30–33s:** Tine places a paw onto the first plank in garden and waits for Tart to match the step. Both then advance slowly.
Camera: 50mm low medium two-shot includes both faces and front paws. Sound: Two gentle synchronized taps; encouraging piano phrase.

**33–36s:** At the bridge crown in garden, Tart glances at Tine, then straight ahead. Their ears lift and tails begin to wag as they keep walking.
Camera: 65mm eye-level moving two-shot; subtle background parallax. Sound: Soft wood taps and fur movement; pizzicato grows warmer.

**36–40s:** Tart and Tine step off the far ramp onto grass beside ball in garden. Tart straightens proudly; Tine gives a little happy bounce.
Camera: 35mm wider side view; small upward camera settle at landing. Sound: Grass pawfalls and one happy woof; score opens into a bright chord.

## 40–50s — Your turn to help

Tart returns the kindness when Tine becomes distracted.

**40–43s:** Tine leans toward fragrant daisies in garden, captivated by the flowers. Tart looks at Tine, then at ball with a knowing smile.
Camera: 50mm medium two-shot; flowers foreground, ball lower center. Sound: Sniff-sniff, leaf rustle; playful flute-like score accent without speech.

**43–46s:** Tart nudges ball gently into Tine’s field of view. Tine notices, turns back, and touches a paw to the ball beside Tart’s paw.
Camera: 65mm close view of noses, ball and paws; tilt up to their expressions. Sound: Rubber brushing grass, surprised sniff; familiar piano motif returns.

**46–50s:** Tart and Tine exchange a delighted look, then gently roll ball together toward the oak tree in garden.
Camera: 35mm lateral follow resumes left-to-right travel; no fast chase. Sound: Two soft happy woofs; paw patter; lively but tender score.

## 50–60s — A little braver together

Their small adventure ends in quiet shared confidence.

**50–53s:** ball settles beneath the oak in garden. Tart and Tine arrive together and slow to a stop, breathing lightly after their small adventure.
Camera: 35mm wide eye-level; oak frames the pair and bridge remains visible behind. Sound: Soft grass steps; piano cadence begins; garden ambience continues.

**53–56s:** Tart and Tine sit with shoulders touching in garden. Tart rests the head lightly against Tine; Tine leans back in return. Tails wag softly.
Camera: 65mm medium two-shot, gentle push; cream and brown faces clearly distinct. Sound: Tiny contented sighs, soft vest fabric; original piano resolves.

**56–60s:** Hold Tart and Tine together under the oak in garden with ball at their feet. Camera eases back to reveal the little bridge they crossed; warm light catches their orange vests.
Camera: 35mm slow pullback; last composition holds during the final second. Sound: Last piano chord rings and fades naturally; birds and breeze continue.
