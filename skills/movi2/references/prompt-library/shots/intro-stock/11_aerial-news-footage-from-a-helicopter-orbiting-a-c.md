AERIAL NEWS TREATMENT — THIS IS THE GOVERNING STYLE
Footage from a gyro-stabilised long-lens camera mounted under a news helicopter, operated by a camera operator inside the aircraft. Broadcast pool footage: functional, unglamorous, recorded to document rather than to impress.
THE HELICOPTER IS FLYING, NOT HOVERING STILL. It holds a slow wide orbit around the square at a constant altitude and a constant distance. The orbit is SLOW: over eight seconds the viewing angle changes by only ten or fifteen degrees. It never circles fast, never banks hard, never changes direction.
THE CAMERA IS STABILISED BUT NOT PERFECT. THE OPERATOR IS RIDING THE SHOT: one slow deliberate reframe during the eight seconds, correcting for the aircraft's movement, arriving a beat late. No whip pans, no snap zooms, no hunting.
