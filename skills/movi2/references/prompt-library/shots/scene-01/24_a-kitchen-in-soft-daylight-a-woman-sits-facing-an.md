A kitchen in soft daylight. A woman sits facing an older man, seen over his shoulder. She holds his
eyes, accuses him in four words, and then her face goes — the expression of someone about to cry,
who does not.

tired drawn features with fine lines around the eyes. Black crew-neck t-shirt under a sleeveless
cream off-white quilted vest with a wide flat collar. Seated. 100% matches the reference.
<<<char_dad>>>: older man, seen only as the back of his head and one shoulder in the extreme foreground

Geography, materials and daylight only.

<<<char_wife>>> is seated facing him, occupying the right two thirds, framed from mid-chest up, her
head slightly right of centre and high in frame.

the right edge — all dissolved into soft bokeh.
Only these two people exist in the room.

framing, wardrobe, lighting, background, colour.
<<<char_wife>>> seated, upright, shoulders square, chin level. Her eyes are open and locked on
<<<char_dad>>> to screen-left. Her mouth is closed, jaw set. She is holding something in.
Her eyes are clear and DRY — no water, no glassiness, no redness.
<<<char_dad>>> foreground shoulder dark and still at the left edge.
No hands in frame. No other person.

ACTION TIMING — THE PERFORMANCE
0:00–1:60 — She holds his eyes without speaking. Her jaw is set. She draws one breath in through
the nose — the chest lifts, the nostrils flare slightly — and holds it. Her chin lifts a few
millimetres. Nothing else moves. The silence is loaded.
1:60–3:20 — She speaks, low and tight, without raising her voice. She says exactly:
"You decide who lives!"
Her eyes stay locked on him for the entire line. The delivery is controlled and pressed down — the
volume stays low while the strain rises, the voice thinning and hardening rather than getting
louder. The last word cracks slightly in her throat.
Her head does not move. The accusation is carried entirely by the eyes and the jaw.
3:20–4:20 — She holds his eyes in silence for a full beat after the line. Nothing changes yet.
This is the accusation landing.
4:20–5:60 — The composure fails, but the crying never arrives. In one continuous movement her face
takes the shape of someone on the very edge of tears and stops there: the inner ends of her
eyebrows lift and draw together, a small vertical crease forms between them, the forehead creases
faintly, the muscles at the corners of her mouth pull downward and outward, her lower lip presses
up against the upper, and her nostrils widen. Her chin dimples very slightly under the pressure of
the mouth. Her throat works once as she swallows hard. Her breathing goes shallow and uneven.
Her eyes stay OPEN and DRY throughout — no water forms, no film builds, no glassiness, no redness.
The whole collapse is muscular. The face is arrested one step before crying and holds there.
5:60–6:60 — She turns her eyes away from him, downward and to screen-right, because she does not
want him to see her face like this. Her head follows a few degrees late. She blinks once, slowly.
The expression does not release and does not progress — it stays exactly where it arrived.
6:60–7:00 — She holds there, eyes down, breathing shallow and audible, the same held expression on
her face. The shot ends with her still on the edge.
She never cries. She never shouts. She never sobs. She never covers her face. She never stands.

PHYSICS
Real facial musculature throughout — the expression moves through connected muscle groups in a
continuous chain, never as a swapped mask and never jumping between poses. Brow, orbital muscles,
nasolabial folds, chin, jaw and throat all move in relation to one another.
The pre-cry expression is built from muscle only: inner brow raise, corrugator pull between the
brows, depressor pull at the mouth corners, mentalis tension dimpling the chin, and the lower lip
pressed up. These arrive together as one continuous chain, reach their peak, and hold without
advancing further.
Skin behaves as skin: fine creases form at the outer eye corners and between the brows where the
muscles pull, the freckles and pores stay consistent.
The eyes remain dry, clear and normally reflective for the entire shot. No tear film thickens, no
lid margin turns glossy, no water pools, no tear forms and none falls. No eye redness, no swelling.
Breathing is visible in the chest and shoulders throughout, and becomes shallower and more rapid
after 4:20.
Loose hair strands at her temples move very slightly with her breath and her head movement.
Lip movement matches the spoken words precisely and naturally, with the tongue and teeth behaving
correctly. The jaw opens and closes with real hinge mechanics.
No rubbery face, no CG morphing, no expression that jumps, no floating hair.

LIGHTING
Soft natural daylight from a window to the front-right, slightly above eye level. It wraps her face
from the right, modelling the cheekbone and the bridge of the nose, and falls off gently across the
left side of her face into soft shadow. No hard edge, no visible shadow line.
A faint cool bounce from the pale wall fills the shadow side very slightly.
Her eyes carry a soft window catchlight high on the right of each iris. That catchlight stays small,
clean and unchanged for the whole shot — it never brightens, spreads or smears.
The foreground shoulder at the left edge is in deep shadow, almost black, and blocks light from
that side.
Palette: cool, desaturated, low contrast, muted greens and greys with skin holding the only warmth.
Gentle halation on the window and the brightest highlights. Fine film grain.
Exposure fixed for her face and unchanged for the entire shot. No fill light, no beauty light, no
key change, no exposure shift, no flicker.

AUDIO
Her voice only, low and close, with the room's soft acoustic around it. Breathing audible
throughout and shallower after 4:20. One swallow. Quiet domestic room tone — a fridge hum far off,
faint traffic through the glass.
The only spoken words in the entire shot are: "You decide who lives!"
No sniffing, no sobbing, no crying sounds of any kind.
No other dialogue. No reply. No music. No score. No subtitles.
