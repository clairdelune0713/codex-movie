SCENE CONTEXT
A small orange handheld device sits flat on a worn pale wood table. Camera locked. On the black display, the white rabbit icon performs a stop-motion animation — scaling up and back down in hard discrete steps. Complete silence.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
Small rectangular device with rounded corners, bright orange matte plastic body, black glass display panel on top face, white minimalist rabbit logo centered on display, circular metal camera lens on front edge, small orange button top-left corner. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Camera position: table-grazing low angle, shooting horizontally across pale scratched wood surface toward device front edge.
Foreground: pale wood table extending toward camera, soft texture visible.
Midground: centered, flat on table, display panel facing upward at low angle toward camera.
Background: blurred soft window light, fully out of focus, no readable detail.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
First frame: perfectly centered horizontally and vertically. Device flat and still on table. White rabbit icon centered on black display, sharp and clearly readable, at its base size. Identical composition to reference image. No hands. No people.

FORMAT MODE
Single static locked take. No camera movement of any kind. Tripod-locked. 5 seconds.

OPTICS
85mm equivalent. Shallow depth of field — display surface and rabbit icon in sharp focus. Foreground table and background window softly blurred. Focus locked. No rack focus. No exposure change.

CAMERA
0:00–5:00 — Completely locked static frame. No drift, no tilt, no push, no breathing, no handheld wobble. Frame 1 and frame 150 are identical in composition.

ACTION TIMING
0:00–5:00 — The white rabbit icon scales up and back down in a stop-motion loop. Each state change is an instant hard cut between fixed sizes — NO interpolation, NO easing, NO tween, NO smooth scaling. The icon is never between two sizes; it is only ever at one or the other.

Three states:
State 1 — base size, 100%. Held.
State 2 — enlarged, 120%. Held.
State 3 — back to base size, 100%. Held.
Loop restarts.

Each state holds approximately 0.4 seconds. Full cycle approximately 1.2 seconds, repeating seamlessly across the 5 seconds with no visible reset frame and no pause between cycles.

Scaling is uniform and centered — the icon grows and shrinks around its own center point, staying perfectly centered on the display at every size. It does not drift, does not shift position, does not move toward any edge.

The icon never rotates, never tilts, never skews, never stretches. Proportions are identical at both sizes — only the overall scale changes. No squash, no distortion of any kind.

The maximum size is only 20% larger than the base — a subtle change, not a dramatic zoom. The icon stays well within the display panel at its largest.

PHYSICS
Device completely stationary. No micro-vibration. Table static. Background light constant — no passing shadows, no ambient variation. Display brightness constant throughout.

LIGHTING
Soft cool diffuse window backlight. Display self-illuminated — clean white rabbit on pure black panel. No glare on display glass. No reflection hotspot. Orange body in soft ambient, material texture subtly visible. No flicker. No light variation across 5 seconds.

AUDIO
Absolute silence. No SFX. No ambient room tone. No music. No surface noise. Zero audio output.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
Camera locked for entire duration — zero movement. Device stationary. Only the rabbit icon animates.
Scaling is hard stop-motion — instant size changes, no interpolation, no easing, no tweening, no smooth growth.
Maximum scale is 120% of base size — subtle, not dramatic.
Icon stays perfectly centered at every size — no drift, no positional shift.
Icon never rotates, tilts, skews, stretches or distorts. Proportions identical at both sizes.
Loop seamless — no visible reset, no flash, no pause between cycles.
No sound of any kind. Display in sharp focus throughout. No hands. No people. No background movement.
