SCENE CONTEXT
A man stands at a dirty window in cold daylight, smoking, seen in tight profile. He looks out, lifts
his eyes, then exhales slowly. Nothing else happens. One continuous 6-second take.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
<<<char_captain>>>: 40s male, dark medium-length hair swept back off the forehead with loose strands
falling at the temple and over the ear, hair longer at the nape. Full dark beard with grey through
it. Deep-set tired eyes, fine lines at the outer corner. Dark grey-olive shirt with a plain collar.
Seen in near-full profile facing screen-RIGHT for the entire shot. 100% matches the reference.
<<<loc_apt_cap>>>: interior at a window. Dirty glass with visible smears and streaks, a dark maroon-red
painted vertical frame member running down the right of frame, pale grey-green daylight beyond, and
a blurred building facade outside, unreadable. A dark green plant mass low in the midground behind
him. Pale plaster interior wall, dim. Geography, materials and daylight only.

LOCATION MAP
Camera is inside the room, at his eye level, positioned slightly behind his cheek line so he reads
in near-full profile with a little of the back of his head toward camera.
He stands facing the window, which is at screen-RIGHT, 0.5 meters in front of him.
He occupies the LEFT two thirds of frame. The window and its dark red frame member fill the RIGHT
third, dissolved out of focus.
Behind him, mid-left, a dark green plant mass and dim interior, both heavily defocused.
Only one person exists in this shot.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
The first frame is an exact match of the reference frame and does not change from it in any way.
His head fills the left two thirds of the image, in profile facing screen-right, cropped at the top
of the skull and at the collar near the bottom edge. The mass of dark hair fills the upper left
corner and runs off the left edge. His ear, cheek, beard, nose and brow are all readable in profile.
His eye is visible in profile, looking out level through the window.
His lips are slightly parted.
The window fills the right third: dirty pale grey-green glass, the dark maroon-red vertical frame
member, the blurred facade beyond.
A thin thread of smoke rises from below the bottom edge of frame and drifts up past the window.
No hand, no cigarette and no object is visible in the frame at any point.
Exactly one person. No one else, no duplicates, no reflections of other people in the glass.

FORMAT MODE
Single continuous uncut take. 6 seconds. No cuts. Real-time motion, no slow motion.
Camera near-static — the faintest operator presence only, no visible handheld shake.
2.35:1 widescreen.

OPTICS
29° diagonal field of view, short telephoto lens character (85mm equivalent), camera 1 meter from
his face. Very shallow depth of field: his eye, cheekbone and the near side of his beard razor-sharp,
the back of his head already softening, the window glass and the red frame member dissolved into a
smooth wash, the facade outside completely unreadable.
Focus is locked on his eye for the whole shot and never moves. No zoom, no rack focus, no lens drift.

CAMERA
Lens at his eye level, level axis, effectively locked.
0:00–6:00 — The frame holds. Only the faintest breathing drift, a few millimetres, and one small
settle around 3:30. No reframe, no push, no tilt, no pan. Frame one and the final frame are
identical in composition. The camera does not react to any movement.

ACTION TIMING
0:00–1:50 — He is still, looking out through the glass, his eye level, at something beyond. He does
not move. His lips stay slightly parted. He breathes twice, visible only in the small movement at
his throat and the collar. The only thing moving in the frame is the thin thread of smoke rising
from below the bottom edge and drifting up past the window on the right.
1:50–2:30 — HIS EYES GO UP. The eye visible in profile travels upward, tracking something higher
outside the window. This is an eye movement first — the iris and the lid move, and only then does
the chin follow, lifting a few degrees a fraction later. The head movement is small and lags behind
the eyes. He does not tilt his head back.
2:30–2:60 — He holds there, eyes up, chin slightly lifted. Nothing moves.
2:60–4:40 — He exhales, slowly and continuously, through his parted lips: a thick unhurried stream
of smoke leaves his mouth toward the window, expands, and breaks into turbulence as it meets the
glass. It rolls back on itself and drifts upward across the right of the frame. The exhale lasts
nearly two seconds and thins out at the end. His face does not change while he does it — no squint,
no sigh, no expression. His eyes stay up.
4:40–6:00 — He holds. Still looking upward and out, chin still slightly lifted, lips still parted.
Smoke continues to drift across the right of the frame and slowly disperses. He blinks once, slowly,
around 5:40. Nothing else moves. He is still there at the final frame.
He never speaks. He never turns toward camera. His face is never seen from the front. He never
lowers his eyes again.

PHYSICS
Smoke has real fluid behaviour throughout: the thread rising from below the frame moves in a slow
column, wavers, and breaks into turbulence. The exhaled stream leaves the mouth with momentum,
expands as it slows, meets the glass and rolls back, then drifts upward and disperses. It is
side-lit by the window so it reads as volume rather than haze, and it is denser at the mouth and
thinner as it spreads. The rising thread continues underneath the exhale and after it.
Real facial musculature — the eye movement, the chin lift and the blink move through connected
muscle groups, never as a swapped pose. The eyes lead the head and the head follows a fraction
later. Skin behaves as skin: pores, the sheen on the cheekbone and the nose, individual beard hairs
and grey strands readable at this focal length in the raking window light.
Loose hair strands at the temple move very slightly with his breath.
Breathing visible at the throat and the collar throughout.
No floating smoke, no static smoke, no smoke detached from his mouth, no rubbery face, no CG
morphing.

LIGHTING
Cold overcast daylight from the window at screen-right, soft and diffuse, slightly above his eye
level. It is the only source.
It rakes across his profile from the right: the bridge of his nose, the brow ridge, the near
cheekbone, the front edge of the beard and the parted lips are lit and modelled, and the light falls
off rapidly across his face so that everything from the ear backward — the jaw hinge, the mass of
hair, the collar — sits in deep shadow. The back of his head reads almost black against the brighter
window.
A very weak cool bounce from the pale interior wall lifts the shadow side by a fraction.
The window itself is the brightest area of the frame — flat pale grey-green, blooming slightly, with
the dirty streaks on the glass catching the light. The dark maroon-red frame member is the only
saturated colour in the image.
The smoke is lit from behind by the window and glows where it crosses the light.
Palette: cold, desaturated, low contrast, muted greens and greys, with his skin holding the only
warmth in the frame. Gentle halation on the window. Fine film grain.
Exposure is set for his lit profile and stays fixed for the entire shot. The window is allowed to
bloom and the shadow side is allowed to crush. No fill light, no beauty light, no key change, no
exposure shift, no flicker.

AUDIO
SFX only. Quiet interior room tone, close and dry. Faint muffled traffic and city through the glass,
distant. His slow exhale, audible and close. The soft shift of fabric at the collar as he breathes.
No dialogue. He does not speak. No music. No score. No subtitles.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
The first frame is an exact match of the reference frame — same framing, same head position, same
window, same lighting, same colour, same lens character.
Exactly one person. No one else is ever visible, no duplicates, no reflection of another person in
the glass.
NO hand, NO arm, NO fingers, NO cigarette and NO object enter the frame at any point. Only his head,
shoulder and collar are ever visible, plus the smoke.
Smoke is present from the first frame, rising from below the bottom edge, and continues throughout.
He stays in near-full profile facing screen-RIGHT for the entire 6 seconds. He never turns toward
camera, never turns to three-quarter or full face, never looks into the lens. His face is never seen
from the front.
He is completely still for the first 1.5 seconds — the only movement in the frame during that time
is the rising smoke and his breathing.
His EYES move up BEFORE the exhale and before his chin lifts. The eye movement leads and the head
follows late. He keeps his eyes up for the rest of the shot.
He exhales only once, and only after his eyes have gone up.
He never leaves his position, never steps forward or back, never touches the glass, never opens the
window.
The camera never moves — no pan, no tilt, no push, no zoom, no rack focus, no reframe, no follow.
Focus stays locked on his eye for all 6 seconds.
The framing at the first frame and the final frame is identical.
Identical exposure and colour from the first frame to the last.
Sharp clarity on his eye and the near side of his face, natural skin texture, stable picture, no
