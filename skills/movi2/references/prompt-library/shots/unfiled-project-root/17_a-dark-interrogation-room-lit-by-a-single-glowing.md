SCENE CONTEXT
A dark interrogation room lit by a single glowing ceiling panel. A man sits down on a transparent
chair set in front of a long white table, then grips the sides of the seat and works himself forward
into the table before settling. One continuous 5-second take, tight three-quarter framing.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
<<<char_captain>>>: 40s male, dark medium-length hair swept back off the forehead with loose strands
falling at the temple, full dark beard with grey through it, deep-set tired eyes, fine lines at the
outer corners. Pale grey collarless linen shirt buttoned at the front, sleeves long. Seen in
three-quarter view, framed from mid-chest up. 100% matches the reference.
<<<loc_interrogation_room>>>: interrogation room. A single large rectangular glowing light panel set into
the ceiling, cold blue-white, the only source. A long white table with a thin dark metal frame. A
transparent moulded polycarbonate chair with a slim dark metal frame and tubular arms, positioned in
front of the table facing it, pulled back from it at the start. Dark grey seamless walls, glossy and
near-black, falling away into darkness at every edge. Pale concrete floor, only faintly lit. Cold
blue-grey palette, deeply crushed blacks. Geography, materials and lighting only.

LOCATION MAP
The chair sits in front of the long side of the table, facing it, in the normal way — <<<char_captain>>>
sits down on it with the table directly in front of him and closes the gap to it.
Camera is 2 meters away, level with the table, positioned off to one side and slightly in front of
him, so he reads in THREE-QUARTER view — his face angled away from the lens toward the table, with
both the near cheek and part of the far cheek visible.
The white table edge runs across the lower part of frame in front of him, its lit surface the
brightest thing in the lower half of the image.
<<<char_captain>>> occupies the centre of frame, framed from mid-chest up. His legs, the seat of the
chair and the floor are all below the bottom frame edge and never visible.
Behind him: dark near-black wall, with the faint edge of the ceiling light panel bleeding into the
top of frame.
Only one person exists in the room.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
First frame: <<<char_captain>>> is already mid-movement, lowering himself onto the chair — his torso
descending, shoulders slightly raised, one arm reaching back and down out of the bottom of frame
toward the chair. He is framed from mid-chest up, three-quarter view, the table in front of him at
the lower edge of frame.
He is not yet seated and the first frame is not a static hold.
No legs, no seat, no floor, no chair back visible — only his upper body and, briefly, his forearms.
Exactly one person. No one else, no duplicates, no reflections of other people in the walls.

FORMAT MODE

Shoulder-mounted handheld, not stabilized, following his movement.

OPTICS

No zoom, no lens drift.

2:20–4:00 — As he works himself forward, the camera reacts a fraction late to each movement: a small

4:00–5:00 — The camera settles and holds on his face, breathing, one last small correction.

Real facial musculature — the small effort in his face and the release afterward move through
connected muscle groups, never as a swapped mask.
No floating motion, no weightless chair, no smooth slide, no rubbery CG movement.

The dark walls behind him give back nothing. Everything beyond him falls to near-black.
Palette: cold blue-grey, desaturated, high contrast, deeply crushed blacks. Fine film grain.
Exposure is fixed for his lit upper body for the entire shot. No fill light, no beauty light, no key
change, no exposure shift, no flicker.

AUDIO

No dialogue. He does not speak. No music. No score. No subtitles.
