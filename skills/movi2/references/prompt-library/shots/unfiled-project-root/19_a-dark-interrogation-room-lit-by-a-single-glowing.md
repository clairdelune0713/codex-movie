SCENE CONTEXT
A dark interrogation room lit by a single glowing ceiling panel. A man walks in, sits down on a
chair set in front of a table, and rests his right forearm on the tabletop. One continuous 5-second
take.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
<<<char_captain>>>: 40s male, dark medium-length hair swept back off the forehead with loose strands at

view facing screen-RIGHT. 100% matches the reference.
<<<loc_interrogation_room>>>: interrogation room with seamless matte panelled walls, fine recessed seams
and softly rounded corners, a panelled floor, and a large flush luminous ceiling panel. A white
table with rounded corners and solid slab legs. A simple chair set in front of it, facing it.
IN THIS SHOT THE ROOM IS DARK. The ceiling panel is the only source and it is dimmed low — the walls
are unlit and read as deep charcoal to near-black, the floor is barely visible, and only the white
tabletop still catches light. This is NOT the bright high-key version of the room. Geography,
materials and layout only; the lighting comes from the LIGHTING section below.

<<<char_captain>>> occupies the CENTRE-LEFT of frame.

The right side of frame beyond the table corner is empty dark wall.
Only one person exists in the room.

Never stabilized, never gimbal-smooth, never a dolly feel. No pan away, no tilt down to the table,

This is NOT the bright high-key version of the room — the walls, floor and background stay dark
