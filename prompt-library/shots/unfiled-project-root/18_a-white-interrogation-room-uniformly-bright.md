SCENE CONTEXT
A white interrogation room, uniformly bright. A man sits down on a chair set in front of a table,
grips the sides of the seat, works himself forward, and settles. One continuous 5-second take, very
tight three-quarter framing on his upper body.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
<<<char_captain>>>: 40s male, dark medium-length hair swept back off the forehead with loose strands

three-quarter view, framed from mid-chest up. 100% matches the reference.
<<<loc_interrogation_room>>>: a white interrogation room. Seamless matte white panelled walls with fine
recessed seams and softly rounded corners, a white panelled floor, and a large flush luminous
ceiling panel filling most of the ceiling. A single small recessed grille and indicator set flush
into the far wall. A white table with rounded corners and solid slab legs. A simple white chair set
in front of it, facing it. Everything in the room is white and matte. Very high key, extremely low
contrast, almost no shadow anywhere. Geography, materials and lighting only.

LOCATION MAP
The chair sits in front of the long side of the table, facing it, in the normal way —
<<<char_captain>>> sits down on it with the table directly in front of him and closes the gap to it.
Camera is 2 meters away, positioned off to one side and slightly in front of him, so he reads in
THREE-QUARTER view — his face angled away from the lens toward the table, with the near cheek and
part of the far cheek visible.
Framing is very tight on him: mid-chest up. The TABLE IS NOT VISIBLE — it sits below the bottom
frame edge and never enters the shot. His legs, the chair seat, the chair back and the floor are
also all below the bottom edge and never visible.
Behind him: flat white wall, one continuous surface, with at most a single faint recessed seam line.
Only one person exists in the room.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
First frame: <<<char_captain>>> is already mid-movement, lowering himself onto the chair — his torso

toward the chair. He is framed from mid-chest up, three-quarter view.
He is not yet seated and the first frame is not a static hold.
The frame contains only his head, shoulders and upper chest against flat white wall. No table, no
chair, no floor, no furniture edge, no equipment.
Exactly one person. No one else, no duplicates, no reflections of other people.

Shoulder-mounted handheld, not stabilized, following his movement.

OPTICS
