SCENE CONTEXT
A vote has just closed in a large international assembly chamber. Hundreds of seated delegates react with nothing: papers are gathered, one delegate removes his glasses, another checks his watch, figures walk out up the side aisle while the session is still running. The most consequential vote in human history is covered like a routine budget session by an unattended broadcast crew.

The cuts are house-system switcher cuts between three fixed cameras already installed in the chamber, not directed coverage. Every camera stays in the rear half of the room and faces forward toward the podium, so screen direction never reverses.
No fade, no crossfade, no dissolve, no transition effects. No subtitles, no music, no burned-in titles.
