SCENE CONTEXT
An elderly Japanese scientist stands behind a glass aquarium on a white pedestal inside a sterile white laboratory. The tank contains three large koi carp — massive, densely patterned, filling the small tank volume. Camera opens tight on his face, aquarium out of focus in foreground. He slowly leans down to tank level. Camera descends with him. Rack focus shifts to the glass and water — his face visible behind the koi, refracted and distorted through water, the large fish bodies crossing the plane between camera and his eyes.

<<<char_scientist2>>>: Elderly Japanese male, approximately 70s, short silver-white hair, thin-framed rectangular glasses, lean weathered face, calm precise expression. Grey open kimono-cut jacket, black underlayer, wide-leg black cargo trousers. 100% matches the reference.
<<<loc_lab2>>>: Sterile all-white laboratory interior, viewed from outside through a continuous floor-to-ceiling glass panel wall that spans the full width of frame. The glass wall has vertical metal mullions dividing it into three equal panels. Recessed fluorescent ceiling panels arranged in a regular grid — bright, flat, clinical. White lab benches with scientific glassware and equipment lining both side walls. White epoxy floor. Center of room: a white rectangular pedestal supporting a rectangular glass aquarium with a pale blue-white backlit rectangular panel mounted on the wall directly behind it. The room is seen entirely through the glass wall — slightly teal-tinted, cool, overexposed white ambient. Small goldfish currently in tank — replaced in this shot by <<<prop_karp>>>. 100% matches the reference.
<<<prop_karp>>>: Three large koi carp — substantial body mass, thick and heavy proportions. Distinct markings: one red-and-white kohaku pattern, one orange-black-white showa pattern, one predominantly white with black patches. Large rounded bodies, broad paddle fins, visible scale texture, barbels at mouth. They fill the tank volume densely — bodies nearly touching the glass on all sides. 100% matches the reference.

Phase 2 midground: <<<char_scientist2>>> face behind water and koi — refracted, distorted, fragmented by fish bodies passing across his face.
Background: pale blue-white backlit wall panel behind tank, white lab walls, lab benches with equipment on both sides — always soft.

LIGHTING

Entire scene viewed through glass wall — adds faint teal cast to ambient. Phase 1: scientist face in flat frontal lab light, clinical. Phase 2: his face behind water and koi lit by blue-white backlight — cool, underexposed, refracted. Koi colors vivid against cool blue-white water. No warm ambient sources. No shadows on white walls.

AUDIO
SFX only. No music.
SFX: faint laboratory ventilation hum, subtle fluorescent electrical tone, quiet internal tank filter current, occasional soft water displacement as koi move — low bubbling, no splashing. Clinical and minimal.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
