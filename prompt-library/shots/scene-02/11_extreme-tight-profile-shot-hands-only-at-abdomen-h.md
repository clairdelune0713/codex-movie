SCENE CONTEXT
Extreme tight profile shot — hands only, at abdomen height. Both hands suspended in air, palms facing downward, fingers slightly spread. Wet from the shower just ended. A continuous involuntary nervous tremor runs through both hands for the entire duration. No water running. No costume visible. No face visible. Hands alone in red ambient.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
<<<char_captain_bath>>>: Use only hand anatomy — lean male hands, approximately late 30s–40s, moderately veined, short-trimmed nails, no rings. Wet — residual shower water. 100% matches the reference.
<<<loc_bathroom>>>: Deep saturated red shower room — monochromatic flat red. All shower heads off. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Camera: extremely close — approximately 20–30cm from hands. Lens at exact hand height, strict profile angle — 90 degrees from body axis, shooting horizontally across the hand plane.
Hands: centered in frame, both hands at abdomen height, suspended without support. Palms facing downward. Fingers naturally spread — not splayed wide, not clenched. Slight natural curl at finger tips from tension.
Background: deep red wall fully dissolved — pure soft red bokeh. Nothing readable.
Frame: wrists to fingertips only — no forearms beyond wrist, no torso, no costume visible at any point.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
First frame: both hands centered, palms down, at abdomen height. Strict profile framing — hands seen from the side, fingers extending screen-right. Wrists at screen-left edge of frame, fingertips at screen-right. Hands already trembling from frame 1 — small rapid involuntary oscillation, vertical axis primary, slight lateral component. Skin wet — water film visible on back of hands, faint water beads between knuckles, one slow drip forming at fingertip. Background pure soft red. No forearms. No torso. No costume.

FORMAT MODE
Single continuous uncut take. 5 seconds. No cuts. Camera locked — no dolly, no movement of any kind.

OPTICS
135mm equivalent. Extremely shallow depth of field — near hand edge sharp, far hand and background dissolve into soft red. Focus locked on nearest hand dorsal surface and knuckle plane. Wet skin texture and individual water beads sharp. No rack focus.

CAMERA
0:00–0:05 — slow dolly-in, really slow.

CHARACTER BEHAVIOR
0:00–0:05 — Both hands suspended at abdomen height, palms down, trembling continuously. Tremor is nervous and involuntary — small rapid oscillation, approximately 8–12 cycles per second, low amplitude. Not a shake, not a shiver — a fine uncontrollable quiver that cannot be stopped. Fingers carry the tremor to their tips — fingertip movement slightly larger amplitude than wrist. The hands do not move from their position — they hold their height and orientation while trembling within it. No gripping. No clenching. No reaching. Just suspension and tremor.

PHYSICS
Hands wet from shower — water film on dorsal surface, beads collecting between knuckles and at finger joints. One or two slow individual drips from fingertip edges during 5 seconds — single drops detaching under gravity, falling below frame. Tremor amplitude: approximately 3–5mm vertical oscillation at fingertips. Water beads on skin surface move with the tremor — micro-oscillation visible on each bead. Wet skin catches red ambient — faint sheen on knuckle ridges and finger dorsal surface. Tendons visible under skin at back of hand from held suspension and tension.

LIGHTING
Monochromatic deep red ambient — no white, no cool tones. Side lighting from above-right — knuckle ridges and back of hand dorsal surface catch red ambient highlight. Valleys between fingers and knuckle joints fall into deeper crimson shadow. Wet skin holds red specular on highest points — knuckle peaks, fingertip pads from the side. Water beads catch red light as tiny luminous points on skin surface. Background pure soft red bokeh — hands silhouetted in profile against dissolved red. No fill. No rim from behind.

AUDIO
SFX only. No music. No background music of any kind.
SFX: post-shower silence. One or two single water droplets falling from fingertips to wet floor — isolated, slow, irregular. Faint distant ventilation hum. No running water. No dialogue. No music. No fabric sound. No movement sound.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
Strict profile — 90 degrees from body axis, held for entire 5 seconds. Hands only in frame — no forearms beyond wrist, no torso, no costume visible at any point. Palms facing downward throughout. Tremor present from frame 1 — continuous, involuntary, nervous. Never stops, never increases dramatically — sustained fine quiver. Skin wet throughout — water film, beads, one or two slow drips from fingertips. No running water in frame. Camera locked — zero movement. Background pure dissolved red — nothing readable. No extra elements. No subtitles. No music.
POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS — COSTUME LOCK
No costume, swimwear, underwear, or any fabric visible in frame at any point during the entire 5 seconds. Bottom frame edge cuts cleanly above any clothing line — if the bottom of frame approaches the waist area, it cuts before any fabric enters. The lowest visible elements in frame are the wrists and the underside of the hands only. No textile, no waistband, no fabric edge, no costume corner at any frame position. If any clothing would naturally fall within the framing, the frame cuts above it. Skin and hands only below the wrist line.
